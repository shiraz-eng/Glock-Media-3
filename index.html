FAQs

Common Questions

What makes Glock Media different from other agencies?

Unlike traditional marketing agencies focused on ads, we operate at the intersection of content strategy, audience psychology, storytelling, and platform algorithms. We build scalable media assets, not just isolated content pieces.

Do you work with new channels or only established ones?

We work with both. For new channels, we handle everything from niche validation and branding to content planning and publishing systems. For existing channels, we focus on scaling, optimization, and revival strategies.

What platforms do you support?

We support YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat, and X (Twitter). We also help with multi-platform content repurposing to maximize reach and audience acquisition.

How do you measure success?

We track CTR, audience retention, watch time, subscriber conversion, engagement patterns, and revenue. Every decision follows our Kill / Fix / Double Down framework based on real data.

What is your pricing model?

We offer flexible pricing based on scope and scale. Contact us for a custom quote tailored to your specific needs, whether it's a single service or full content ecosystem management.
